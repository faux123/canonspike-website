About

28+ years building software across domains that couldn't be more different — flight control systems at BAE Systems, AR headsets at DAQRI, hyperloop vehicles at Virgin, and enterprise platforms serving global sales teams. I shipped several top‑10 mobile applications in their day, and learned what it means to build for millions of people at once.

Today I lead engineering and product at Seismic, focused on the platform that helps modern sales organizations work smarter. The breadth of that journey shapes every decision I make: technically rigorous, product‑minded, and always oriented toward real‑world impact.
